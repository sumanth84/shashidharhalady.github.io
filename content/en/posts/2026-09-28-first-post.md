This is the first article on this site. New articles are added by
committing a Markdown file to `content/en/posts/` (or `content/kn/posts/`
for Kannada), then pushing — the site rebuilds and republishes
automatically.

This site supports both English and Kannada (ಕನ್ನಡ) articles, served as
separate language sections with their own navigation.
